Lot Size Basics

Lot size is a standardized way of measuring trade size, especially in forex markets.

A standard lot represents a large amount of currency, while mini and micro lots represent smaller amounts. Smaller lot sizes are typically safer for beginners.

Using the correct lot size helps traders manage their exposure to the market. Larger lot sizes mean larger price movements will impact your account more significantly.

Understanding lot sizes is important because it allows you to control risk and scale your trading appropriately.
