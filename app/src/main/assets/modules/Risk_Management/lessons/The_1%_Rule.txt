The 1% Rule

The 1% rule is a common guideline used by traders to manage risk. It states that you should never risk more than 1% of your account on a single trade.

This rule helps protect your account from large losses and ensures long-term survival in the market.

By limiting risk per trade, traders can handle losing streaks without significant damage to their account.

The 1% rule promotes discipline and consistency, which are essential for long-term success.
