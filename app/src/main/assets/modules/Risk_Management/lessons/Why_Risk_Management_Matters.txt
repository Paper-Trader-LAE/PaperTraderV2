Why Risk Management Matters

Risk management is one of the most important aspects of trading. It determines how much you can lose on a trade and how you protect your overall account balance.

Many new traders focus only on making profits, but experienced traders focus on controlling losses. Even a profitable strategy can fail if risk is not managed properly.

Without risk management, a few bad trades can wipe out an entire account. With proper risk control, traders can survive losses and continue improving over time.

Successful trading is not about winning every trade. It is about managing risk so that losses are small and profits are allowed to grow.
