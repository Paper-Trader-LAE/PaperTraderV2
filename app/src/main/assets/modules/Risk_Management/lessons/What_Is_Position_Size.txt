What Is Position Size?

Position size refers to how much of an asset you are buying or selling in a trade. It directly affects how much money you gain or lose.

Larger position sizes increase both potential profits and potential losses. Smaller position sizes reduce risk but also reduce profit potential.

Choosing the correct position size is essential for controlling risk. It should always be based on your account size and how much you are willing to risk on a trade.

Proper position sizing ensures that no single trade can significantly damage your account.
