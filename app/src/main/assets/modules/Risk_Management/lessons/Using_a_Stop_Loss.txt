Using a Stop Loss

A stop loss is a tool that automatically closes a trade when the price reaches a certain level. It is used to limit losses.

Setting a stop loss protects your account from large losses if the market moves against you. It allows you to define your risk before entering a trade.

Without a stop loss, losses can grow quickly and become difficult to control. Many beginner traders avoid using stop losses and end up losing more than expected.

Using a stop loss is a key part of disciplined trading and risk management.
