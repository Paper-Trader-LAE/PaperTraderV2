Risk-to-Reward Ratio

Risk-to-reward ratio compares how much you are risking on a trade to how much you expect to gain.

For example, risking $10 to make $30 results in a 1:3 risk-to-reward ratio. This means your potential reward is three times your risk.

Good traders focus on maintaining favorable risk-to-reward ratios. Even if they lose multiple trades, one winning trade can recover those losses.

This concept helps traders stay profitable over time, even with a lower win rate.
