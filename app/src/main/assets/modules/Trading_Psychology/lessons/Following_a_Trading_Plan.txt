Following a Trading Plan

A trading plan is a set of rules that guides how a trader makes decisions.

A strong trading plan includes what markets to trade, when to trade, what setups to look for, how much to risk, where to place a stop loss, and when to take profit.

Following a trading plan helps remove emotion from trading. Without a plan, traders are more likely to make random decisions based on fear, greed, or excitement.

A plan also helps traders review their performance. If a trade loses but followed the plan, it may still be considered a good trade. If a trade wins but broke the rules, it may create bad habits.

Consistency is one of the most important parts of trading. A trader cannot improve if they constantly change strategies or ignore their rules.

In PaperTrader, users can practice building discipline by treating simulated trades as if they were real. The goal is to develop habits that can transfer to real trading environments.
