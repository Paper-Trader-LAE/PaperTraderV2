Handling Losing Streaks

A losing streak happens when a trader loses several trades in a row. Every trader experiences losing streaks, even experienced ones.

The key is not to avoid losses completely, because that is impossible. The key is to control losses so they do not damage the account or the trader’s confidence.

Many traders make losing streaks worse by increasing position size, revenge trading, or abandoning their strategy. These emotional reactions can lead to even larger losses.

A better approach is to slow down, review each trade, and determine whether the losses came from normal market behavior or poor decision-making.

If the trades followed the plan, the trader may simply be experiencing a normal drawdown. If the trades broke the plan, then the trader should identify what went wrong and adjust.

Handling losing streaks requires patience and discipline. The goal is to protect the account long enough to continue learning and improving.
