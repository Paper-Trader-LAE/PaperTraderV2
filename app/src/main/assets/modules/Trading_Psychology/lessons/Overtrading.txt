Overtrading

Overtrading happens when a trader takes too many trades, often without a clear reason or strategy.

Many beginners overtrade because they feel like they always need to be active in the market. They may enter trades out of boredom, excitement, frustration, or fear of missing out.

Overtrading is dangerous because it increases risk. The more trades a person takes without discipline, the more chances they have to make mistakes.

Good trading is not about taking the most trades. It is about taking the right trades. Sometimes the best decision is to wait and do nothing.

To avoid overtrading, traders should have a clear plan before entering the market. They should know what setup they are looking for, how much they are willing to risk, and when they will stop trading for the day.

PaperTrader can help users recognize overtrading habits by reviewing past trades and seeing whether each trade had a valid reason.
