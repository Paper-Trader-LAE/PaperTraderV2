Trendlines

Trendlines are lines drawn on a chart to help identify the direction of price movement.

In an uptrend, a trendline is usually drawn by connecting higher lows. This helps show where price may continue to find support as it moves upward.

In a downtrend, a trendline is usually drawn by connecting lower highs. This helps show where price may continue to face resistance as it moves downward.

Trendlines are useful because they make market structure easier to see. They can help traders identify possible entry points, exits, and trend changes.

A trendline should not be forced onto a chart. It should connect clear price points and make sense visually. If price keeps respecting the trendline, it may be a useful guide.

Traders often combine trendlines with other tools such as support and resistance, moving averages, or candlestick patterns to confirm their decisions.
