Combining Confluences

Confluence means using multiple pieces of evidence to support a trading decision.

For example, a trader may see price near support, a bullish candlestick pattern, and an oversold RSI reading. Each signal alone may not be enough, but together they create stronger confirmation.

Combining confluences helps traders avoid entering trades based on only one reason. This can reduce impulsive decisions and improve trade quality.

Common confluences include trend direction, support and resistance, candlestick patterns, moving averages, RSI, MACD, and market structure.

A good trade setup usually has several reasons behind it. The goal is not to find a perfect trade, but to find trades where the evidence supports the decision.

In PaperTrader, practicing with confluences can help you build a habit of thinking before entering trades. Instead of pressing buy or sell randomly, you learn to ask, “What evidence supports this trade?”
