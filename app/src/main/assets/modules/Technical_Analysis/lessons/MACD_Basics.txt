MACD Basics

MACD stands for Moving Average Convergence Divergence. It is a technical indicator used to help traders identify momentum and possible trend changes.

The MACD is usually made of two lines: the MACD line and the signal line. When these lines cross, traders may use that as a possible sign of changing momentum.

If the MACD line crosses above the signal line, it may suggest bullish momentum. This means buyers may be gaining strength.

If the MACD line crosses below the signal line, it may suggest bearish momentum. This means sellers may be gaining strength.

The MACD also includes a histogram, which shows the distance between the two lines. A growing histogram can suggest increasing momentum, while a shrinking histogram can suggest weakening momentum.

MACD should not be used by itself. It works best when combined with trend direction, support and resistance, and other forms of confirmation.
