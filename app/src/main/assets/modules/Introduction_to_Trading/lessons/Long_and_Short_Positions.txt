Long and Short Positions

In trading, you can profit from both rising and falling markets by taking long or short positions.

A long position means buying an asset with the expectation that its price will increase. If the price rises, the trader can sell the asset at a higher price and make a profit.

A short position means selling an asset with the expectation that its price will decrease. In this case, the trader sells first and buys back later at a lower price, profiting from the difference.

Short selling allows traders to take advantage of downward market movements, which is something that traditional investors cannot easily do.

Understanding long and short positions is essential because markets do not always move upward. Being able to adapt to both directions gives traders more opportunities.
