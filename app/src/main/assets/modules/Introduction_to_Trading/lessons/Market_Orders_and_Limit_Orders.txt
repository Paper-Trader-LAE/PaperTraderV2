Market Orders and Limit Orders

There are different types of orders that traders use to enter and exit the market.

A market order executes immediately at the current market price. This is the fastest way to enter a trade, but the exact price may vary slightly.

A limit order allows you to set a specific price at which you want to enter a trade. The trade will only execute if the market reaches that price.

Market orders are useful when you want immediate execution, while limit orders are useful when you want more control over your entry price.

Understanding order types helps traders execute their strategies more effectively and manage risk more precisely.
