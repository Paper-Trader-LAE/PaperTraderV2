Trading vs Investing

Trading and investing are two different approaches to participating in financial markets.

Investing focuses on long-term growth. Investors typically buy assets and hold them for months or years, allowing their value to increase over time. They rely on company performance, economic growth, and long-term trends.

Trading, on the other hand, focuses on short-term price movements. Traders actively buy and sell assets over shorter periods, such as minutes, hours, or days. Their goal is to profit from small price changes.

Investing is generally considered less risky over long periods, while trading can involve higher risk due to frequent decision-making and market volatility.

Both trading and investing require discipline and knowledge. However, traders must be more active, constantly analyzing the market and managing their positions.

Understanding the difference between these two approaches helps you decide how you want to participate in the market.
