Volatility Basics

Volatility refers to how much the price of an asset moves over a period of time. High volatility means prices are changing rapidly, while low volatility means prices are relatively stable.

Volatility is important because it affects both risk and opportunity. Higher volatility can lead to larger profits, but it also increases the potential for losses.

Traders must adjust their strategies based on market volatility. For example, during high volatility, traders may use wider stop losses to avoid being stopped out too quickly.

Understanding volatility helps traders manage risk and choose the right conditions for their trading strategy.
